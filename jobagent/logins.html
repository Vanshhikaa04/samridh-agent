Site logins ← Jobs

Some sites only let signed-in people apply. Log in once here and the agent reuses your session on that site only. Your password is never seen or saved: you type it into the site's own page.
  1. Press Open login window: a browser window opens on this computer.
  2. Sign in there (finish any verification).
  3. Come back here and press Save session. Jobs waiting for that site are queued automatically.
